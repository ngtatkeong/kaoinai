What is DG and AI?

DG and AI (Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence) is the unifying technical practice and platform architecture that cleans, structures, and protects enterprise database estates so that artificial intelligence models and autonomous agents can operate reliably without hallucinations, data leakage, or silent schema drift. In the agentic AI era, AI cannot function without DG, and DG cannot scale without AI. 1. The First Law of Agentic AI: When Data is Junk, Frontier AI Will Also Be Junk

Across the global technology industry, hundreds of millions of dollars are invested in frontier Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, and Gemini. Yet empirical research from RAND Corporation (2024) and Gartner benchmarks that over 80% of enterprise and SME AI initiatives fail to transition beyond proof-of-concept.

The breakdown is almost never algorithmic. The breakdown is structural: AI models reason strictly over the data they are fed. If an enterprise database contains:

  • Cryptic, unindexed table schemas with zero metadata definitions;
  • Contradictory revenue or customer records across disjointed departmental silos;
  • Silent schema shifts where data engineers drop or rename columns without updating downstream pipelines;
  • Unmasked customer PII (NRIC, MyKad, NIK, credit cards) that gets ingested directly into external LLM prompts;

Then the AI agent will inevitably hallucinate false calculations, produce invalid SQL, or commit critical statutory privacy violations. Data Governance (DG) is not a bureaucratic afterthought—it is the prerequisite foundation of artificial intelligence.
