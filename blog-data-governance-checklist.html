Governing Regulator: Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC Singapore)

Primary Legislation: Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) & Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act. Applies to all private sector organizations collecting, using, or disclosing personal data in Singapore.

⚠️ Statutory Penalties: Up to 10% of annual turnover in Singapore (for turnover > S$10M) or S$1,000,000
✓

1. Accountability Obligation & DPO Designation

PDPA § 11(3)

Appoint at least one designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) and make the DPO's business contact information publicly accessible (e.g. on website footer or privacy policy).

💡 Technical Action: Publish DPO email, establish internal data protection policies, and document personal data inventories across all databases.
✓

2. Notification & Consent Obligation

PDPA § 13–20

Notify data subjects of the explicit business purposes before collecting personal data, obtain affirmative opt-in consent, and provide a clear mechanism to withdraw consent.

💡 Technical Action: Implement cookie/data consent banners with timestamped database logging of consent state and purpose scope.
✓

3. Purpose Limitation Obligation

PDPA § 18

Collect, use, or disclose personal data strictly for purposes that a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances.

💡 Technical Action: Restrict analytics teams from joining sensitive customer PII with unconsented external marketing datasets.
✓

4. Accuracy Obligation

PDPA § 23

Make reasonable efforts to ensure personal data is accurate and complete if it is likely to be used to make decisions affecting the individual or disclosed to another organization.

💡 Technical Action: Deploy 4D data quality monitors to catch duplicate records, missing values, and corrupted data syncs in real time.
✓

5. Protection Obligation (Security Safeguards)

PDPA § 24

Implement reasonable security arrangements (technical, administrative, physical) to prevent unauthorized access, collection, use, copying, alteration, or disposal.

💡 Technical Action: Enforce AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, automated PII column masking, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
✓

6. Retention Limitation Obligation

PDPA § 25

Cease retention of personal data documents or purge identifiers as soon as the purpose for collection is no longer served and retention is no longer necessary for legal or business reasons.

💡 Technical Action: Set up automated retention policies and scheduled data anonymization/purge jobs for inactive customer tables.
✓

7. Transfer Limitation Obligation (Cross-Border)

PDPA § 26

Ensure that personal data transferred outside of Singapore receives a standard of protection comparable to that under the PDPA (e.g. via Standard Contractual Clauses or Binding Corporate Rules).

💡 Technical Action: Audit third-party SaaS tools and cloud hosting regions (AWS/GCP/Azure) to verify data sovereignty and transfer clauses.
✓

8. Access & Correction Obligation

PDPA § 21–22

Provide individuals upon request with access to their personal data and records of how it has been used or disclosed in the past 12 months, and correct inaccuracies promptly.

💡 Technical Action: Maintain immutable data access audit logs and automated lineage to identify all downstream repositories containing the user's data.
✓

9. Mandatory Data Breach Notification

PDPA § 26B–26E

Notify PDPC within 3 calendar days (72 hours) if a data breach results in, or is likely to result in, significant harm, or affects 500 or more individuals. Affected individuals must also be notified as soon as practicable.

💡 Technical Action: Establish automated incident response alerts when unusual mass-export or anomalous query volume triggers in the warehouse.
✓

10. Data Portability Obligation

PDPA § 26F

Transmit an individual's personal data to another organization in a commonly used machine-readable format (JSON, CSV) upon authorized request.

💡 Technical Action: Implement structured export endpoints for customer profiles and transactional history.
✓

11. Data Intermediary / Processor Oversight

PDPA § 4(2)

Ensure that data intermediaries processing data on your behalf (vendors, cloud providers) have signed Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with strict security guarantees.

💡 Technical Action: Maintain vendor risk logs and verify SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certifications across all database integration vendors.
🏛️

Governing Regulator: Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi (JPDP Malaysia)

Primary Legislation: Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709) and the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act A1727). Applies to all commercial transactions processing personal data in Malaysia.

⚠️ Statutory Penalties: Fines up to RM 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 3 years
✓

1. General Principle & Lawful Consent

PDPA Act 709 § 6

Process personal data only with explicit consent from the data subject, or where processing is strictly necessary for the performance of a contract.

💡 Technical Action: Maintain central consent registries and ensure sensitive data (health, biometric, religious) has explicit written authorization.
✓

2. Notice & Choice Principle (Bilingual Requirement)

PDPA Act 709 § 7

Provide clear written notice in both Bahasa Melayu and English detailing the data collected, purpose, third-party disclosures, and contact details for data inquiries.

💡 Technical Action: Deploy dual-language privacy policies and in-app terms on all Malaysian consumer and SME touchpoints.
✓

3. Security Principle & Direct Processor Liability

Act A1727 § 9

The 2024 Amendments impose direct legal obligations on Data Processors to implement robust technical and organizational security measures to prevent loss, misuse, or unauthorized access.

💡 Technical Action: Ensure database connections, ETL jobs, and data warehouse instances enforce RBAC and end-to-end encryption.
✓

4. Mandatory Data Protection Officer (DPO) Appointment

Act A1727 § 12A (2024 Amendment)

Qualifying Data Controllers and Processors must appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO) who is ordinarily resident in Malaysia (present ≥ 180 days/year) and notify the Commissioner.

💡 Technical Action: Register the appointed DPO with JPDP and empower the DPO with access to internal audit logs and lineage maps.
✓

5. Mandatory Data Breach Notification Regime

Act A1727 § 12B (2024 Amendment)

Notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as soon as practicable / within 72 hours of a suspected breach if it poses significant harm to data subjects. Failure to notify carries fines up to RM 250,000.

💡 Technical Action: Configure automated anomaly tripwires on production databases to detect unauthorized bulk queries or exfiltration immediately.
✓

6. Cross-Border Data Transfer Due Diligence

Act A1727 § 129

The old whitelist has been replaced. Organizations must conduct internal assessments to ensure recipient overseas jurisdictions have substantially similar data protection laws.

💡 Technical Action: Document cross-border data flows and enforce data residency configurations in ASEAN cloud regions (e.g. MY/SG).
✓

7. Retention & Data Integrity Principles

PDPA Act 709 § 10–11

Destroy or permanently delete personal data once the commercial purpose is fulfilled, and take reasonable steps to ensure data is accurate, complete, and not misleading.

💡 Technical Action: Run automated data deduplication routines and automated data purge workflows on customer CRM tables.
🏛️

Governing Regulator: Lembaga Pelindungan Data Pribadi (Kominfo / Lembaga PDP Indonesia)

Primary Legislation: Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP). Fully enforceable across all public and private entities operating in Indonesia.

⚠️ Sanctions: Administrative fines up to 2% of annual revenue; Criminal penalties up to 6 years prison & IDR 60 Billion
✓

1. Legal Basis & Explicit Consent (Dasar Pemrosesan)

UU PDP Pasal 20

Processing must be grounded in valid legal basis: explicit written/digital consent, contractual obligation, legal obligation, vital interests, or legitimate interest.

💡 Technical Action: Store digital consent records with cryptographic timestamping in your Indonesian customer database.
✓

2. Data Subject Rights (Hak Subjek Data Pribadi)

UU PDP Pasal 5–15

Support statutory rights including: Right to information, Right to access and copy, Right to rectify/correct, Right to erasure/deletion (Hak Penghapusan), and Right to data portability.

💡 Technical Action: Build automated "Right to be Forgotten" deletion scripts that cascade across all relational tables and reporting marts.
✓

3. Mandatory DPO / Pejabat PDP Appointment

UU PDP Pasal 53

Mandatory appointment of a Data Protection Officer (Pejabat Pelindungan Data Pribadi) if processing involves public service, systematic large-scale monitoring, or specific/sensitive data (NIK, health, biometric, financial).

💡 Technical Action: Assign internal compliance lead or certified DPO and integrate DPO review into database architecture changes.
✓

4. Mandatory 72-Hour Breach Notification (3x24 Jam)

UU PDP Pasal 46

In the event of a personal data failure or breach, Data Controllers must deliver written notification within maximum 3 x 24 hours (72 hours) to the PDP Agency and affected data subjects.

💡 Technical Action: Maintain real-time database audit logging to capture IP, user, and affected records in the event of an exfiltration attempt.
✓

5. Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA / Analisis Dampak)

UU PDP Pasal 34

Conduct a mandatory DPIA before implementing high-risk data processing activities, automated decision-making (AI profiling), or large-scale sensitive data integration.

💡 Technical Action: Document risk vectors prior to training internal AI models or connecting large customer data tables to external APIs.
✓

6. Technical Security & Pseudonymization

UU PDP Pasal 35–39

Ensure confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal data using state-of-the-art encryption, access controls, and pseudonymization/masking techniques.

💡 Technical Action: Automatically mask NIK, bank account numbers, and phone numbers in non-production environments.
✓

7. Cross-Border Data Transfer Requirements

UU PDP Pasal 56

Transfer personal data outside Indonesia only if the recipient country has an equal or higher data protection level, or if binding contractual agreements / explicit user consent is secured.

💡 Technical Action: Ensure primary data stores reside within Indonesia (Jakarta cloud regions) or have verified cross-border DPA execution.
